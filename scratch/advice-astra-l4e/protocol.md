# L4-E: inherited credit for forecasting changes in food

Exploratory design, not registration. One public stochastic world, option (a) only. No lineage knows its realized future. A large unknown prior would not break exact flattening if both sides could inherit the same bits.

## Outcome and screen

Use only 3201-3216 for model selection; rank whole configurations by mean late living flies, then listing order. Before evaluating META3 as a treatment or using 3217-3232, require (i) L2best beats FIXED, (ii) oracle d4 exceeds one fly, and (iii) its paired one-sided exact sign-flip p < .05. Define d4 = (O-F)-1.5(B-F). This test after tuning is descriptive. Stop on failure. No changes to this garden, search grid, or decision after seeing scores. A treatment-equivalent flat policy is deliberately in the challenger grid. Do not suppress its scores on the grounds that they preview the treatment.

If the oracle passes, lock META3 to the best hard DIRECT decay at the matched best constant L2's alpha and beta. Tune birth-only alternatives on tuning, including the neutral matched-L2 genotype. Score all locked treatment, control and family winners once on held-out. Require positive lower rungs, positive d4, a one-fly margin above L2best, SHAM3 and EVO-R3, positive inheritance contrast, and positive 2(P11-P01)-3(P10-P00) with at least 11 positive seeds; use exact p < .01 throughout. No cherry-picking another runner-up after holdout. Report that passing a finite list does not establish a hierarchy advantage against all fixed full-state programs.

## Batch culture

One feeding pulse per ten world ticks, 2,000 pulses. The colony sees a synchronous succession of fruit batches. In pulse t, hidden food sign s, its velocity v, and its acceleration a are +/-1. Initial hidden bits are independent fair bits. For the next pulse, acceleration flips with probability .0125; velocity is multiplied by new acceleration and independently slips with probability .06; the food sign is multiplied by new velocity. Each fly draws an independent erroneous taste with probability .07, even if it declines. At price U uniform on [0,1), it eats if the common prediction (1+w)/2 exceeds U. Net energy from a bite is 1{eat}((1+y)/2-U). The random price, clocks, IDs, colony count, true state, and fecundity never reach any non-oracle learner.

There are 64 founders. Every 16 pulses the cohort dies and each parent leaves stochastically rounded max(0,(1.6+4*mean net energy)/(1+N/65)) offspring; stop adding children at safety cap 256, log all hits. Inherit the parent's controller word except L1 weight, which resets to zero. All ordinary and oracle arms face the same seed, pulse and slot random draws. No parents choose offspring feedback. Endpoint: 100 colony sizes at original world ticks 10100,10200,...,20000. This is a pulse-feeding batch culture, not the spatial World4.

## Declared finite machine

Every fly has one unsigned 16-bit controller word: a signed four-bit taste forecast w (15 values, -7/7 to 7/7) and twelve free bits q. The host holds demographic data, inaccessible to the controller. Every bite costs exactly one 16-bit read from an immutable 262144-word ROM, addressed by (word,taste bit,oracle bit): two shifts, two ORs and a lookup, no other online learner computation. Every arm gets the same ROM allocation, common readout, and one birth lookup from a 1048576-word ROM keyed by (q,external mutation byte). Birth resets w. No clocks, auxiliary online code, private floats, cache, probes or per-arm learned tables. The model and all ROM contents can be computed offline, never with realized seed data. Founders have common zero w; sufficient-statistic encodings initialize at their neutral prior. Only ORACLE3 and the full diagnostic get the oracle bit. It is the sign of E[y_next | s,v,a], not a future draw.

L1 can only write w' = quantize(w + alpha*(y*z - w)). All controllers must pass through this channel. L2 estimates observed velocity u=y*previous_y. With current velocity prediction z0, it corrects b=quantize((1-beta)*z0+beta*u), then forecasts next velocity z=orientation*b; b and z0 have seven levels. Zero b when previous_y is absent. L3 compares the last L2 branches +b_old and -b_old to the next observed u. Their squared-loss advantage is 4*b_old*u. It adds proportional signed evidence to an inherited, discounted, saturating 4-bit score. The sign sets the orientation of L2's next update; a zero score uses the matched orientation. SHAM3 deterministically averages the opposite labelled L2 writes (their post-quantization mean is zero). RESET clears only the score at birth. EVO-R3 has the same four score bits, writable only at birth; stable E0 maps to the matched constant. Include all score values as standing variation and mutation probabilities 0, 1/16, 1/4, 1. Factorial P00 = FIXED; P10 = best constant-correction L2; P01 = active meta shadow with the locked FIXED channel applied; P11 = META3.

## Challenger coverage

The source's `grid()` enumerates every cell. Alpha .25, .5, 1 for all grids; FIXED z in {-1,-.5,0,.5,1}; constant L2 beta in {.25,.5,.75,1}, both orientations. Quantized IDBD and AutoStep adapt beta from velocity errors and a sensitivity trace, with two starting rates, three meta-rates, both orientations. RMS normalizes by recent squared velocity error, two decays, three scales, both orientations. DIRECT reads exactly the same inherited validation score and branch forecasts, then writes the transported velocity itself (hard sign or soft gain; three decays and both tie orientations). The hard reader has a fixed, treatment-equivalent joint transition, not a straw comparator. Also give Bayes the full public HMM: recurrent eight-state filters quantized to 4 or 7 probability quanta and exact rolling-window predictors for the last 4, 8, or 12 observed labels. All Bayes readers can inherit their full sufficient-statistic code and project their ideal posterior forecast onto the nearest reachable L1 write, including L1 rounding and inertia. ORACLE3 chooses between the two legal signed L2 updates to minimize next expected taste loss. Tune alpha and beta for the oracle, and show a full-write oracle diagnostic separately. Neither oracle optimizes lifetime fecundity.
