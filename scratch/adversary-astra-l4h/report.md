# L4-H blind adversarial review

The question is whether tuning an adaptive learner's rate beats a strong flat learner, not whether inheritance beats a learner forbidden to inherit. **Do not freeze the current wrapper as a test of that claim.** On a learner whose rate provably does nothing, the wrapper lowers the rate on every pair in a falling-loss trace. The switching Kalman covariance update, in contrast, passes an independent reference check.

**My blind forecast is 3% for passing both practice and the fresh final**, conditional on fixing the controls and scoring, registering the challengers, and getting one independent, in-scope family. This is a judgment, not a probability estimated from runs. There is no distribution over world authors or families from which to calculate it. L3 has a real opportunity if a useful rate persists across births but changes too fast for selection. It must identify that rate from serial, action-selected bites, beat flat learners that compare timescales on the *same* bites, and turn the advantage into population rather than just lower error. I did not run a gate or measure that advantage on the toy world.

## Required fixes before the blind draw

1. **Randomize block order and define which prediction belongs to which rate.** `microverse/l4h-arms.mjs:143-164` always starts high, so age, learning progress, or any time trend can stand in for rate. The first prediction has not received either treatment. At a block boundary the wrapper scores the pre-update prediction, switches rate, and only then calls `L.learn`. With `n=2`, `c=.2`, and initial log-rate zero, the first four *applied update* rates were `+.2, -.2, -.2, +.1`, not two high and two low. In `check.mjs` the diagnostic base ignores its rate and approaches a constant observation; five successive pairs still moved the center from `0` to `-.5`. This is not evidence of an inverted gradient. The direction for two properly attributed losses is correct. Draw `+,-` or `-,+` independently per pair on a dedicated learner RNG. Attribute a pre-update loss to the preceding treated update, skip the untreated first prediction, and schedule exactly `n` updates per sign. Keep loss sums keyed by sign, not by first/second position. Test equal exposure and zero *expected* signed drift on this null in both pair orders. Random order does not eliminate carryover or make different bites counterfactuals. If causal rate sensitivity matters, add same-stream paired shadow learners as a diagnostic before the draw.

2. **Make parity an effective budget, not just an array length.** `idbd` and `autostep` return `D` and `2D` floats even with `inheritL2:false`, although the child discards them. A Kalman base consumes one; its wrapper consumes two. A flat candidate cannot be known to meet *the eventual winner's* cap if different candidates define their own cap by winning. Pick a single maximum `B` before Stage A, or register separate budget strata comparing a wrapped base with the best flat library member under its actual cap. Declare the active floats for every arm, assert the serialized count at birth, and return an empty array when inheritance is disabled. Offer flat learners the spare float for observation-derived state; the paired `REPEATQR` variants below exercise that distinction. Also include a flat static-rate search across the wrapper's reachable range: a fixed improved rate outside the bare grid is not evidence for performance-dependent tuning. If keeping the registered grid as-is, limit the claim to those grid points. Give ordinary and wrapped base factories access to the same kind of seeded private randomness; `makeLearner` currently passes `r` to the wrapper but not to the base (`:131-135,170-174`).

3. **Keep both shams, but do not count damage to a sham as mediation.** NOWRITE matches the perturbation schedule, not the undithered L2 baseline. It is informative about writing the center but toothless about *why* the center moved. RANDSIGN is a bounded inherited random walk; it can be punitive. On a true loss tie it even moves when META3 does not. Both effects are possible, not measured harms in the unknown family. Match phase, tie/no-write events, opportunity count, step sizes, bounds and independent random streams. Report paired `NOWRITE - L2BEST`, `RANDSIGN - NOWRITE`, and `RANDSIGN - L2BEST` with intervals. Predeclare that beating a materially harmed sham cannot by itself support a mechanism claim. Add a flat, observation-independent signed schedule or a matched open-loop annealing control. Report completed pairs before reproduction and the fraction of births with a non-default center. RESET removes direct transmission of the center, not the L2 state the parent's tuned rate helped produce. Gate the RESET contrast if the conclusion specifically attributes an advantage to inherited tuning.

4. **Give EVOR a non-punitive founder option.** The current EVOR has genuine selection, the same base state, one bounded gene, standing variation, and mutation choices including zero. It is a useful control. Every grid point, including `pm=0`, forces uniformly spread founders, whereas every META3 founder begins at the locked center. Mutation size has no effect when `pm=0`. Register a point mass at the center with no mutation (must reproduce L2BEST), plus narrow and full-width standing variation with and without mutation. Tune EVOR independently by the same endpoint. Match the *applied* rate support: the L3 center is clamped but the two probes can extend beyond EVOR's range by `c`. Report founder and reproducing-parent rate distributions. META3 lineages experience selection too; the comparison tests learning plus selection against gene-only selection, not learning against an absence of selection.

5. **Fix the odor catalogue fallback.** `microverse/l4h.mjs:58-72` moves entries between incomplete lists with `pop()`. If all sampled odors are positive with `kinds=32`, the result contains 16 odors, not 32. Plants already holding kinds 16 through 31 can later yield `undefined` bits at a redraw. Build a fixed-length catalogue. If one sign is unavailable after the search cap, sample replacements from observed odors without removing entries or pretending to change their sign. Record realized sign balance and assert that every kind resolves. Alternatively declare rare-sign worlds inadmissible and fail the whole world draw before any arm outcomes are seen. The sign-stratified catalogue itself uses an oracle to construct the *common ecology*, not to label an arm's inputs. Document that sampling when stating the claim's scope.

6. **Calibrate the Kalman scale updates.** The issue in `kalADQ` and `kalSW` (`l4h-arms.mjs:82,103`) is not the covariance loop. Under the implementation's own calibrated Gaussian prediction, `E[tanh(Z^2 - 1)]` is about `-.18842`, not zero. Midpoint integration on `|Z| < 10` at widths `.002` and `.001` agreed to 14 decimal places. Thus the scale shrinks on average even at correct calibration, away from the clamp. In the calibrated two-branch model the posterior-reset-weighted expectation is the reset prior probability times that same expectation. Recenter the bounded score, or use `1/sqrt(2) - exp(-e^2/(2*S))`, which is centered at the Gaussian model and remains bounded for outliers. Test calibrated innovations before comparing rates; this is a model check, not an assumption that the blind family is Gaussian.

7. **Reject incomplete or mismatched evidence before scoring.** The scorer that appeared during review (`microverse/l4h-score.mjs:12-25,57-74`) compares row count with a header, then overwrites duplicate `(armName,seed)` keys. It derives the seed list from the surviving FIXED rows. A duplicate is not a substitute for the missing seed. The hash check skips absent paths; a matching label is not proof that a row used the locked arm. Require the exact declared seed-by-config product once each, finite integer `popSum`, a positive common `nLate` supplied by the runner, matching lock configs, the exact mandatory dependency hashes (including scorer/stats), and an explicit seed list covering the two disjoint practice ranges. Use a deterministic tie break in Stage A rather than worker arrival order. Do not infer `nLate` from the first row's `popSum/pop`: extinction can produce `0/0`. For a 1.5-times *gain* claim require a positive `L2BEST - FIXED` denominator; equality can give `Infinity`. Keep the registered per-seed integer contrast `2*M3 - 3*L2 + F` and the one-sided exact sign flip. Exactness of the computation is not proof that an arbitrary asymmetric contrast distribution has a sign-symmetric null.

## Frozen challengers

`classf.mjs` exports JSON-safe `DEPTH2_EXTRA` and `register(A)` for the runner's per-worker registry. Both the bare dispatch and the real wrapper use those factories. Every `rateInit` is a log-rate; `setRate` changes an adaptive statistic's update, not just the prediction weights. A fresh child always has `w = 0`. No family reads the world module or accesses a seed, tick, parent weights, shared object, or other fly.

| Family | Update and declared L2 rate | Stage A grid | Active inherited floats |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `DIRRLS` | Directional forgetting RLS; `rate` updates log observation variance from bounded innovation scale. Fixed forgetting alone would be depth 1. | `lambda={.94,.99}`, `rate={.03,.2}` | 1 |
| `SIGNGAIN` | Normalized LMS; signed gradient agreement with a trace changes the global log gain at `rate`. | `rate={.05,.25,1}` | 1 |
| `RMSADAM` | Bias-corrected momentum and error RMS. On signed-bit inputs every coordinate's squared gradient is identical. `rate` sets the RMS memory, not the L1 step. | `step={.15,.6}`, `rate={.02,.15}` | 1 |
| `REPEATQR` | Full Kalman; `rate` updates log process variance. Adjacent tastes of identical odors provide a separate estimate of observation variance. It can include drift between bites. | `carryR={false,true}`, `rate={.05,.3}` | 1 or 2 |
| `SHAREKF` | Three independent fixed-q Kalman experts. Tempered predictive likelihoods and fixed-share mixing set output weights. `rate` sets the evidence weight. | `rate={.3,1}`, `share={.01,.08}`, `R=1` | 2 |
| `SHAREADQ` | Two independent adaptive-q experts with fixed scale-update speeds. Mixer never changes either expert's parameters, state update, or access to bites. | `rate={.3,1}`, `share=.03`, `R=1` | 1 |

`SHAREKF` uses only depth-1 experts and is Class F under the stated exclusion. **`SHAREADQ` is borderline** if selecting among predictions of adaptive experts counts as rewriting their updates. It passes the literal rule: each expert follows the same trajectory on a fixed tape regardless of mixer weight. Set its eligibility before world creation and report sensitivity with and without it; do not let the final results decide. Both banks transmit output probabilities, not expert weights. `REPEATQR` explicitly spends the extra float on a noise statistic estimated from raw tastes; its one-float sibling learns the same statistic within life but resets it at birth. Vector learners cost O(D) per bite; the covariance learners cost O(D²) with at most three experts.

## What the audit did not find

- **No `kalSW` buffer bug.** After row `i` is complete, all remaining upper-triangle reads use indices greater than `i`. Mirroring the triangle and reusing `px[i]` for the new mean are safe; `w` changes only after the matrix is complete. A dense, non-aliasing two-branch reference using second moments matched all three weights and the inherited log prior over 40 updates (maximum absolute difference `6.7e-16`). This checks the moment-matched algorithm, not exact filtering over every switch history. Hazard is per bite, not per world tick.
- **No privileged label in the frozen learners.** `learn` gets the odor and noisy taste only. Energy and the common movement learner do receive the true food sign indirectly; `skill()` also knows the labels but is diagnostic. No examined sensory learner reads either. Different policies induce different bites even on the same seed. Call this the same observation *interface*, not identical observed tapes across arms. The public readout is linear `w.x`, so the experiment is narrower than a competition between arbitrary predictors.
- **The interface relies on trusted code.** `makeShared` could share a mutable buffer across flies, and the live bit array passed to `learn` exposes object identity and could be modified. Neither the stock arms nor the extras use those channels. Remove the shared hook for this gate, pass a private bit copy, and require pure `world.value(bits)` and `noiseSd()` lookups; only `step` may advance world state. The tested L3 loss is computed from the same pre-update `w,x,y` available to F. Its extra capabilities are the inherited scalar, exploratory schedule and forbidden feedback rule, not secret knowledge of the world.
