# L4-C exploration lock, before measurement

One world. Within each seed run two 10,000-tick common gardens, with opposite hidden seasons in seed-randomized order. New hidden four-receptor patch every 1,000 ticks, new causal pair/sign every 200. Inside a patch the next pair must share one receptor (R) or exclude both (S); the marginal pair distribution is exchangeable. Transition histories can reveal the season; randomization identifies the causal effect of a write, not exclusive access to the season.

The first 5,000 ticks of each garden are identical across arms. On one third (Bernoulli) of eligible first validations, a fair coin assigns an in-place reinforce or spread write. Hold the assignee's alpha unchanged for 120 ticks. Count only first validations of candidates actually proposed after the write and validated within 120 ticks. Zero future production after death. Publish on the deadline, not at initiation or death. Off probe the calibration policy is the same scalar L2 for everyone. No extra bites, simulated shadow assays, or population-wide pooling of probe feedback. Each fly accesses its own and ancestors' records; a child can receive its parent's outstanding result when it matures. Do not open a trial that would cross the assay boundary. Save actual assignments and yields; require identical ordered records and fork state hashes across arms.

At tick 5,000 fork to the assay policy. No further random probes in the assay; this preserves literal record equality. Proposals and validations from the assay are on-policy. The test is in stable post-calibration seasons, not continuous online tracking. Primary endpoint is mean live flies at 50 samples per assay (ticks 5100..10000), 100 samples/seed, not the original uninterrupted late trajectory. The fixed deck, NOREV and factorial deck/shadow also go through common calibration before switching. No raw causal target or season is available to non-oracles.

Lock highest arithmetic mean population, ties in declared order, never best per seed. First lock scalar decay/boost over .006/4 (cap 30), .015/4, .03/12, .06/20, .12/40, .20/40 (otherwise cap 60). On locked scalar, tune multiple fixed-kernel strengths, pair-frequency and transition/overlap memory, and DIRECT. DIRECT sees the same assignment-labeled discounted yield estimates; it boosts eligible overlap or disjoint pairs directly. Include confidence-weighted and hard choices. Test the season oracle at three strengths on the locked scalar, under the same non-probe write interface as META3. Primary tuning screen uses the best oracle and best challenger. Continue only if mean oracle d4 > 1 fly, positive challenger gain and exact one-sided p < .05 (descriptive after selection). Report all rows and stop on failure. Never use another world/seed bank to rescue a miss.

If the screen passes, use the locked oracle strength for META3, deterministic post-clip SHAM, RESET, matched P10 (same scalar and no extra write), and ignored live computation P01 (deck). Tune EVO on the overall locked challenger, including neutral, fixed R/S, standing with no mutation and standing with mutation. Confirm locks, all required contrasts and exact sign flips on 2717..2732 once. Tune only on 2701..2716. The required final is outside this experiment.
