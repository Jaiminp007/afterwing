# L4-F: final lineage-tempo screen, frozen before seed exposure

This is an exploratory decision about mediation, not a new registered final. Do not edit tracked files. Use only 3301..3316 to tune. No META3, SHAM3, RESET, or factorial runs before Step 0 passes. Use 3317..3332 once, only if licensed. No other world variants, banks, or post-score changes. Save all seed endpoints, including extinctions. The independent unit is a world seed, not a fly, deme, taste or generation.

## World and endpoint

Four demes, each founded by 18 flies. 24 discrete generations, 80 public odor/taste pulses per generation. Within deme all flies encounter the same random 12-bit odor, noisy label (5% error), and price uniform on [0,1); a free diagnostic taste is available after every choice, even if the fly declined. A fly accepts at its pre-taste L3-V `tastePredict` probability above price; food profit = true 0/1 quality minus price if accepted, zero otherwise. Hidden food rule is one of 66 unordered pairs with either sign, acting by Walsh parity. Before each pulse after the first, resample pair and sign uniformly with hazard 1/64 (fast) or 1/640 (slow). Redraw can reproduce the previous rule. Two pairs of neighboring demes receive opposite initial tempos at random. Each pair swaps tempos independently with probability 1/8 at generation boundaries. Hazards, not realized switch counts, differ tenfold. No learner sees a tempo, rule, payoff, neighbor history, or future draw (except true current tempo for ORACLE).

Ecology is a batch-feeding assay, not a replication of spatial WorldV. Keep the actual `WorldV.tastePredict`, `tasteLearn`, mask buffer, admission, repeated mature-mask boost, cap, proposal law and inheritance of alpha. Use a fresh kind ID every pulse (each encounter supplies evidence). Structural masks and taste weights reset at birth. Each adult leaves stochastic-rounded max(0,0.9+12*mean profit)/(1+adult deme size/12) juveniles, then dies. Each destination keeps its local brood size. With probability .05 per slot a migrant replaces the local child. Pick two distinct foreign demes uniformly; the one with greater realized brood per adult supplies a uniformly sampled juvenile (first wins ties, skip empty donors). This adds between-deme productivity selection to local differential reproduction, identically in every arm. No artificial abundance floor or cap. Endpoint P = mean total live newborns after generations 13..24 inclusive, 12 censuses. Log migrations, births, validations, realized redraws and stage counts.

## Class and oracle

Lock by largest arithmetic mean P over all 16 tuning seeds; ties use enumeration order, never per-seed maxima. FIXED = better of uniform and shuffled deck, both with L1 and no L2. NOREV has no L1. Parameter grid k in nested order delta {.006,.06,.30}, beta {4,20}, cap 30 and floor .02. Six constants. ORACLE evaluates all 6 x 6 ordered (slow k,fast k) maps by running each policy in the ecology, including constant diagonal cells reused exactly. Pick the best whole map. No bit identities supplied. This optimizes the declared grid map on the tuning bank, not all possible policies or all lifetimes.

- EVO: every deme has exactly three founders of each k; genotype inherited, newborn mutates to a uniformly drawn k with probabilities 0,.03,.15,.40. All adults face the same local and migrant-pool selection as every other arm. Also include all six constant genotypes as the no-variation/no-mutation fallback when ranking EVO.
- Surprise-reset: each of six substrates; reinitialize alpha before its ordinary validated write when a newly validated pair received less than {.5,1.5} times uniform probability under the prior, including normalization over 66 pairs.
- IDBD-style: six initial substrates, meta step {.05,.5}; logit delta adjusts by the gradient of the newly validated pair's log probability, using an inherited per-bit sensitivity trace through alpha decay and capped boosts. Clamp delta to [.001,.6]. These two families update once per new validation batch using its first newly valid mask. Ordinary META still writes *all* mature eligible masks each normal write.
- Finite-history Bayes: H={8,16,32} recent local tastes. Exact filtering under the public 2-tempo x 66-pair x 2-sign model for a uniform window prior. Account for 1/8 tempo flips at generation boundaries and tempo-specific redraw before the next observation. Use only tastes preceding the proposal. Pass predicted pair posterior directly to the same L1 draw, mixed with uniform mass {0,.10}; no alteration of L0, K, validation or feeding. A nonmigrant can retain its local parent's last H observations; a migrant starts with an empty record. All policies can recognize birth/dispersal. No pooled latent-state or future-taste access.

Best of class is the best whole setting from constants, EVO, surprise, IDBD and Bayes. Report every family winner and every config x seed in the raw artifact. Step 0 requires positive mean best-minus-FIXED, ratio of mean gains >=1.5, and strictly positive paired d4 on at least 11/16 seeds ("clear majority"; same two-thirds convention as the ladder). d4=(O-F)-1.5(B-F). Descriptive one-sided exact `docs/paper/stats.mjs` signflip on 2*sumO-3*sumB+sumF with grid 1, i.e. units of 1/24 fly. Report spread; the p-value does not correct for model selection. On failure close the line, no alternative run. Probability of a final occurring and passing under the stop rule then equals zero; counterfactual power is not identified.

## Conditional held-out test (no tuning of META3)

L3 transmits six discounted reward weights only, with no bit or pair indices. The fly samples a k by .10 uniform exploration plus .90 softmax(weights). At an L2 validation batch, credit only the k used for the previous prediction, if any, with +1 for a hit, -1 for a miss, after discounting all weights by .9. A hit means the top-mass pair, drawn uniformly among exact ties immediately after the preceding scored L2 write, is among the newly validated admissions now. Track first validation per admission instance, never count repeated boosts on the same instance as independent successes. Select the next k after scoring, use it in ordinary L2 decay and boosts, install the next ticket after that write, hold k until the next event. Pending forecasts are not inherited. RESET clears only L3 weights at birth; SHAM3 replays another deme's META3 k trajectory, matching generation and pulse with rank mapped to a donor fly, rotated by one deme, cyclic fallback if donor empty. SHAM has its own L2 and validation. P01 disables L2 actuation but computes ignored L3 from shadow writes (must be identical to FIXED); P00=FIXED, P10=locked constant, P11=META3.

If licensed, run all family winners, FIXED, NOREV, ORACLE, META3, RESET, SHAM3 and P01 on each held-out seed once. Require positive d4 against locked class winner; positive matched factorial 2(P11-P01)-3(P10-P00) on >=11 seeds; mean margins >=1 fly over class winner, SHAM3 and EVO, positive META3-RESET, constant-FIXED and FIXED-NOREV; one-sided exact p<.01 for all. Only claim mediation versus named comparators, never superiority to any flattened program.
