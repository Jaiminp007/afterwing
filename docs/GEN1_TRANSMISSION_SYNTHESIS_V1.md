# GEN1 Transmission Synthesis V1 (2026-11-04) — what 10 scored worlds say about birth inheritance

## Question
VISION.md milestone 5 asks for population studies with explicit models
and controls; milestone 2 asks for persistent adaptation across
changing tasks. The transmission arc tested one explicit model —
birth-transmitted controller weights (exact vs noise-0.05/0.01 vs
naive init) — with matched controls across colony ecologies. All
worlds single-attempt, fresh banks, frozen plans, negatives preserved.

## Lines scored (closed; banks spent, no rescoring)
- HEIR/HEIR2/HEIR3 (exact birth inheritance; frozen/learned newborns):
  copy out-survives naive 0.331/0.261/0.373 vs 0.105/0.188/0.156.
- CARRY×5 + ERODE×3 + HOME + CUE×2 (same-agent cross-task/return/cue):
  skill survives foreign tasks (fractions 0.57–1.43), dissolves under
  same-world immersion (0.11, 0.25), is cue-bound (relabels kill it).
- MUT/MUT2/MUT3/MUT4 (noise dose at birth + through gauntlet): 0.01
  cost-free at birth (+0.0089), 0.05 cost-free at birth (+0.0081),
  gauntlet fractions 0.62–1.05 — interference taxes noise, birth is free.
- MUTHOME/MUTHOME2 (mut gauntlet + return): pooled frac 0.72 GO-by-dust
  → 0.35 NOGO; 1/2 pairs carry each bank.
- MUTSAME (shared colony): survival gap +0.029 NOGO; intake +0.0035
  clears both pairs; ~98% mortality floor.
- MUTHEIR (heir ecology, mut−naive): survival +0.018/+0.006 fail, intake
  +0.0026/+0.0048 clear; copy 0.211 descriptive, no floor.
- BIRTHCARRY (birth × novel task): copy B-gaps +0.0015/−0.0017 NOGO.
- COPYMUT (copy−mut head-to-head): +0.077/+0.008 NOGO; naive calibration
  holds; mut out-eats copy.
- MUTLOW (0.01 dose, heir ecology): mut01−naive +0.186/+0.065 NOGO;
  copy−mut01 +0.126; intake clears both.
- SHORTCOL/SHORTCOL2 (900-tick window): V1 GO (+0.389/+0.249 copy,
  +0.300/+0.108 mut); V2 NOGO (+0.301/+0.042 copy, +0.288/−0.096 mut;
  ceiling on pair 2).

## Convergent findings (all pair-stable unless noted)
1. **Intake edge is law-like**: mut/copy out-eat naive on 8/8
   bank-pairs across shared colonies, heir colonies, birth probes,
   windows, and both noise doses. Never reversed.
2. **Deaths are always energy (starvation), never age**: the colony
   ecology is boom-bust; costs are flat across arms, so the edge is
   front-loaded intake, not efficiency.
3. **Born<450 all die** (0.00, 90+ offspring, 3 banks, all arms):
   colonies are two-phase (death zone + censored tail); pooled survival
   mixes selection with censoring.
4. **Fate benefit is bank-scale-conditional**: 1792-colony copy−naive
   gaps split pairs in every line; the 900-tick window stabilizes V1
   (4/4 clear) but not V2 (ceiling compresses pair 2, mut reverses).
   Pooled ordering copy > mut > naive holds on both window banks.
5. **Transmission is task-bound and practice-fragile**: nothing crosses
   to novel tasks (BIRTHCARRY flat; CUE relabels kill); same-world
   practice dissolves edges (ERODE 0.11, HOME 0.25, MUTHOME2 −0.02).
6. **Dose matters in level, not kind**: 0.01 narrows the fate deficit
   (pooled +0.126 vs +0.012) without stabilizing it; intake holds at
   both doses; birth is free at both doses.

## VISION mapping
- Milestone 5 (population, explicit models + controls): NARROWED-POSITIVE.
  Birth inheritance works as a phenomenon (intake skill transmits
  reliably; fate transmits unreliably, exact > mutated > naive pooled).
  Co-presence without mechanism is inert (MIX neutral, COHAB miss);
  redistribution is ecological, not agentic (GIVE/DOSE GO, ACT void).
  No claim beyond these bounds.
- Milestone 2 (persistent adaptation across changing tasks): POPULATION
  NEGATIVE alongside same-agent positives (RETAIN 0.91, CARRY×2). Birth
  carries skill forward in time but not across tasks.
- Milestones 1/3/4: untouched by this arc (lifetime-learning benefit +
  reset loss stands as dev-grade; RSI C-series closed NOGO; richer
  bodies still gated).
