## License (all must hold; any fail = no license, no freeze)

(a) Probe-time mechanism (R15/R16): the intervention must operate on,
or be evaluated through, probe-time behavior — what the agent DOES
during transfer (re-acquisition, exploration, transfer dynamics,
closed-loop verification). Weight-matrix state at D is evidence,
never the intervention locus: centering, distributional, split, and
normalized readouts are CLOSED as a family on evidence, not taste.
(b) No phase-gating (R13): history-idiosyncratic price rules out
phase-targeted mechanisms. The rationale must be history-adaptive or
readout-global, stated in the freeze, or it is curve-fitting.
(c) New locus or new channel (C17-bar (a)): the mechanism must touch
something R8–R10 never moved — not the background, not the
representation, not the routing, not the readout norm. Re-tuning,
re-weighting, or re-combining closed instruments is a variation,
not a rationale.
(d) Falsifier with teeth (program standing rule): paired transfer
bars (≥5/8), a mechanism readout that can miss independently of
transfer (dissociations are publishable, not embarrassing), and the
difference-based acquisition gate (R8 lesson, R9/R10 precedent).
(e) Single attempt, no rescoring (program standing rule).
