# POPULATION DIRECTION V1 (analytic, zero worlds — track not opened)

Date: 2026-11-02. Banked for a future VISION-5 line. No license, no
bank, no code, no execution. Two biological precedents + one search
paradigm, all from live literature (R780–R781).

## Precedents (stay two, don't blend one)

- de Belle & Sokolowski 1987 (R780, title record): rover/sitter
  foraging strategies are heritable alternatives — nature maintains
  the polymorphism rather than converging on one mixture.
- Brockmann 2001 (R781, title record): alternative strategies and
  tactics as a standing cross-taxa design pattern.
- Reading: the go-vs-stop problem class is solved in nature by
  coexisting variants under selection, not by one blended head.
  The B-series tried to be both policies in one learner and closed
  four deep; the population direction tries two policies in one
  ecology.

## Paradigm (quality diversity)

- Pugh et al. 2019 (R781): MAP-Elites / Novelty Search with Local
  Competition — archive diverse high-performers, local competition
  as the control, elites as the checkpoints.
- Fit: bounded (archive), checkpointable (elites), controlled
  (explicit competition rules) — open-endedness the frozen-plan
  discipline can adopt. The M-POP transmission-mechanism space
  (closed twice) is the substrate this would build on, not
  re-litigate.

## Opening conditions (not a plan)

A population line opens only with: a fresh frozen plan, a fresh
bank, and a falsifier distinguishing "selection maintains both
policies" from "one policy fixates" (fixation = the null the line
must beat). Until then this doc is a bookmark, not a queue jump.
